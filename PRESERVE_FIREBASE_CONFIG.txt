IMPORTANT BEFORE COPYING v5.5 OVER YOUR PROJECT

Your working Firebase configuration is stored in:

app\firebase-config.js

Back up that file before replacing project files.

After copying v5.5 into your repository, restore your backed-up
firebase-config.js so the app remains connected to your Firebase project.

Do not post or commit passwords. The Firebase web configuration is public
project-routing data, but keeping your configured file out of replacement
steps avoids accidentally returning to blank setup values.
