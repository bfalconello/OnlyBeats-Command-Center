# OnlyBeats v4.4 — College Football Universe Mega Update

## Combined releases

This release combines the planned v4.1 through v4.4 features:

- Favorites & Watchlists
- Team Profiles
- Season Tracker
- Conference Dashboards

## Favorites & Watchlists

- Favorite unlimited teams
- Favorite conferences
- Watch individual games
- Favorite-team game list
- Ranked-watch preference
- Shared favorite state across the app

## Team Profiles

- Team selection
- Loaded schedule
- Loaded-game record
- Current ranking when available
- Connected team stats when available
- Prediction history
- Favorite-team toggle
- Direct game navigation

## Season Tracker

- Overall season record
- Weekly performance
- Monthly performance
- Conference performance
- Ranked-game record
- Favorite-team record
- Best and worst week
- Winning and losing streaks
- Exportable season report

## Conference Dashboards

- Conference team list
- Live, upcoming, and final conference games
- Conference prediction history
- Favorite-conference toggle
- Team Profile links
- Game links
- Clear provider boundaries for unavailable standings and leaders

## Data boundary

OnlyBeats displays loaded provider data and locally saved prediction history.
It does not invent official standings, team statistics, or conference leaders
when those feeds are unavailable.

## Installer

Expected output:

`dist/OnlyBeats-Setup-4.4.0.exe`
