# OnlyBeats v2.7.1 — Unlimited Combo Builder

## Added

- Prediction Combo Maker inside Prediction Center
- No software-imposed leg limit
- Winner, spread, and total prediction legs
- Add, remove, and reorder legs
- Draft autosave
- Save, edit, duplicate, and delete combos
- Combined confidence using geometric mean
- Low, Medium, High, and Very High risk labels
- Duplicate and contradiction warnings
- Per-leg pending, correct, incorrect, and push status
- Combo settlement from final game scores
- Saved-combo summary and history

## Performance note

There is no application limit on the number of legs. Very large combinations can take longer to render depending on the computer.

## Regression checklist

- Prediction Center still loads
- Combo Maker appears inside Prediction Center
- Unlimited legs can be added
- Draft survives navigation and restart
- Conflicting selections show warnings
- Saved combos can be edited and duplicated
- Final games settle winner, spread, and total legs
- Existing predictions, cloud, Windows installer, and production checks still work
