# OnlyBeats Command Center v0.5.0 — Intelligence Center

## Added
- Current-slate ranked-team board
- Ranked matchup collection
- Live/final upset-watch signals
- Favorite-team intelligence alerts
- Scoreboard-derived Game Signals feed
- Provider coverage and readiness panel
- Honest empty/provider states for full polls, standings, editorial news, and player availability

## Data integrity
This release never invents rankings, standings, headlines, or availability information. Rankings are shown only when supplied for teams on the loaded scoreboard.

## Upgrade
No database migration is required. Existing favorites, settings, Saturday Wall filters, and Team Hub preferences remain local.
